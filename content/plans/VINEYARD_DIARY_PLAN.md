# Vineyard Diary Plan

One evergreen, bilingual Wine Talk post collecting the monthly AYIW entries as Evyatar's field diary. Decided with the user 2026-10-19; nothing built yet.

## Decisions

- **Name:** "Vine, Barrel, Bottle: Evyatar's Vineyard and Winemaking Diary". Replaces "A Year in Wine" (which collides with the Year in Wine customer retrospective). The monthly email series takes the same name.
- **Vibe:** a hobby-expert field diary. Evyatar knows wine from the ground up; it is not a pitch for his own wine (the shop carries only a label or two, briefly).
- **Layout:** the newest entry pinned at the top, then a strip of month links, then a rolling twelve months in forward order (oldest first, ending with the newest entry, labeled "Most recent entry"). Entries are dated with month and year ("October 2026"). Each entry has paragraphs and an image.
- **Monthly update:** the new entry replaces the same month from the year before (expanded with this year's real notes, not just swapped), moves to the end of the list, becomes the pinned top section, and the month strip shifts forward.
- **Sticky:** the post is sticky at the top of Wine Talk.
- **Links in:** the AYIW email CTA lands at the top of the post (button text along the lines of "Read the whole diary"); the About page (`/about-evyatar/`) links to it where it mentions his vineyard and winemaking.

- **Starting point:** the diary opens in spring, March 2026 (bud break). Library entries exist for June to October 2026; March, April and May are backfilled from Evyatar's own notes on that season (a few lines each, shaped into entries). November to February are written live this winter, so the diary reaches a full rolling twelve months next spring. Planting is a one-time event, so it belongs in the intro story rather than a monthly entry.
- **Intro:** a short opening paragraph framing the post as Evyatar's field diary (new writing).
