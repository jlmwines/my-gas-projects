The Israeli wine region where it all began

Long before anyone talked about Israeli wine regions, the coast was Israel’s wine country on a serious scale. This is where the ports were, and wine made close to a port could travel the whole Mediterranean. At Yavneh, a huge winery complex around 1,500 years old was built to produce wine for export.

The modern chapter starts here too. In the 1880s Baron Edmond de Rothschild chose the coast to restart the local wine industry, and by 1892 Carmel had built its cellars in Zichron Ya'acov. Some of those cellars are still in use today.

I wanted to show you how the sea does the cooling where there's no altitude, and why the coast's old-vine Carignan is worth seeking out.
