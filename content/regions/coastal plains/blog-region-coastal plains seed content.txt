Coastal plains
While today not considered ideal for winemaking as we have low altitude and high humidity
The sea has a tempering effect with cool sea breezes cooling down the air

Historically - in ancient times the largest wine presses were found here (including Yavneh, which was huge), perhaps because of proximity to ports and therefore trade. 
At least one of the ancient varieties - named Yael - used in commercial wine today was found near ashkelon 

Also in relatively recent history - Baron rothschild’s attempt to restart the local industry happened in zichron.
As a result of history and of inertia, many vineyards are planted there still. Many of Israel’s older vines (many of them being Carignan) are planted there.


Once studies showed that the coastal plains were less suited for certain wines, many of the grapes planted there are now grapes that deal better with heat , like carignan, syrah, marselan and others
