Golan heights
Is a plateau that rises in altitude from south to north
Elevation from around 500-1200 meters (fact check)
Wild, beautiful and heavily agricultural
Mainly volcanic soils - in the south more limestone and more volcanic as we go further north where there are 3 dormant volcanic sites


Recent history -
Early 70s  - cornelius Ough came from UC Davis and said the Golan would be good also for wine grapes (there were many apple orchards) 
First planting was 1976 - originally all grapes were sold to carmel winery
At some point the growers decided to break the contract with the winery and start their own winery
Golan heights winery was started and made their first wine 1983 - a glorious year for humanity
Why are we so focused on this one winery?? - because it made a difference.
They also had a winemaking school for other wineries and growers.
Today there many wineries all throughout the golan from south to north
