## Performance: traffic (complete months Sep vs Oct; Aug and Nov partial, not compared)

- **Organic search sessions fell, HE more than EN.** EN 497 → 368 (-26%), HE 342 → 200 (-42%). Weekly organic peaked the week of 09-06 (135 EN / 113 HE), declined through 10-18 (58 / 36), and ticked up the week of 10-25 (78 / 45).
- **Organic is converting less, not just arriving less.** Organic key events 19 → 8 and organic revenue ₪9,957 → ₪2,678 (-73%), much steeper than the session drop. Pages/session 3.1 → 2.4; bounce 49% → 46% (flat). This is the one number that may not be seasonal alone: holiday-gift intent ending would explain part of it, but not obviously all.
- **Search visibility dropped with it.** GSC clicks 728 → 511 (-30%), impressions 33,588 → 27,218 (-19%), average position 8.8 → 9.4. Fewer impressions plus slightly worse position, so this is demand and ranking, not only click-through.
- **All traffic (Organic Social excluded)** 1,486 → 1,153 sessions (-22%), key events 30 → 22, revenue ₪14,719 → ₪11,391. Non-organic traffic held up better than organic (all-traffic revenue fell 23%; organic revenue fell 73%), so about three quarters of October's revenue (₪8,713 of ₪11,391) came from non-search channels; the export doesn't split which. The week of 10-25 (189 sessions) is the lowest week in the 12-week window.
- **Meta-ads interference was large and is now removed:** 1,536 Organic Social sessions in August (the week of 08-09 alone drops from 1,391 to 510). The August spike earlier reviews read as growth was mostly this.

**Read:** traffic and search visibility are down from a holiday-season September, as expected seasonally; organic revenue fell much harder than organic traffic. Whether October is below a normal October can't be said until year-over-year exists.
