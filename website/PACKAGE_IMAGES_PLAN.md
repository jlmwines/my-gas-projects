# Package Images Plan

Replacing the six Occasion Packages product images with a consistent naïve Israeli folk-art set. In progress: the user is generating images in Canva from the prompts below (several done, approved); upload to WooCommerce not started.

## Decisions

- **Packages get the folk-art set.** The current images are generic line-art tabletops that look alike; the folk-art style gives each package a place and a story. User approved the direction and first results (2026-10-19).
- **Value bundles keep their current images.** A top-down flat-lay redesign was tried and judged a decline: side-on bottle lineups read as "a case of wine" at thumbnail size, label variety signals a curated mix, and label style already distinguishes tiers. Fake AI label text is invisible at thumbnail size.
- **Sure Bets drops the slot-machine "winning combination" image** (gambling plus alcohol is off-tone).
- **BBQ is set on a home balcony**, not a park (home entertaining fits better), kept distinct from Winning Whites by city view, golden hour, and red wine.

## Prompts

Every prompt = shared style block + scene line. Generate Cheese Please first, lock it, and attach it as a style reference in Canva for the other five.

**Shared style block:**

> Square illustration in naïve Israeli folk-art style, inspired by Reuven Rubin and Nahum Gutman: flat colors, simple unfussy shapes, warm hand-painted feel. Palette: terracotta, ochre, cypress green, Jerusalem stone, deep wine red, pale sky blue. Wine bottles drawn as plain unlabeled shapes. Cheerful and inviting. No people, no text, no logos.

**Scenes:**

- **Cheese Please** — A rustic wooden table on a sunlit Jerusalem-stone terrace, with a wheel of cheese and wedges, olives, figs, labneh in a small clay bowl, and two wine bottles. Cypress trees and bougainvillea behind.
- **Shabbat Shalom** — A Friday-evening table by an arched Jerusalem-stone window at sunset. Two lit candles, a braided challah under an embroidered cloth, a silver kiddush cup, and two red wine bottles. A warm golden glow over the stone city outside.
- **BBQ (Al Ha-Esh)** — A home balcony at golden hour with a low charcoal grill (mangal), skewers and a little smoke, a table set with pita, bowls of salads and tahini, and two red wine bottles with glasses. Potted geraniums and herbs along a stone railing, Jerusalem rooftops and water tanks in the warm evening light behind.
- **Red Wine Lover** — A cozy evening corner by a Jerusalem-stone wall, with a clay pot of hearty stew, crusty bread, a bowl of dark grapes, and two red wine bottles with full glasses. A deep blue sky and a lit window.
- **Winning Whites** — A sunny balcony table overlooking the Mediterranean, with grilled fish, a bright chopped salad, lemons, and two pale white wine bottles with glasses. Sea and sky in pale blues, a striped awning above.
- **Sure Bets** — A long courtyard table set for a crowd under a grapevine pergola, with many glasses, shared plates of mezze, and a mix of red and white wine bottles. Festive, welcoming, easy.
